# Continuous inventory origin rules

Rule version: 15.1. Inventory origin is separate from the nine economic income categories.

1. Confirmed manual decisions and existing audited links are authoritative.
2. Actual personal external Market or paid Contract acquisitions take priority over an item catalogue.
3. Matching Mining quantities, bounded own PI recipes and delivered industrial jobs support production provenance.
4. Only a fully observed current source period permits generic residual rules.
5. Ambiguity, missing evidence or contradictions remain unknown or conflict.

## Lots and disposal

Opening and entry stock is `OPENING_STOCK`, with no invented historical purchase price. Separate lots survive physical stack merging and splitting. Disposal uses FIFO ordered by acquisition UTC and stable source identity. Integer quantities and cent costs use BigInt; the final consumed portion preserves the last cost cent. Unknown costs are not zero.

Market purchases use actual transaction quantity times exact unit price. A related journal remains evidence of the same central payment, never an additional expense. Incoming paid personal item-exchange Contracts use an exact negative context payment. Same-type positions can share its cost by quantity; mixed types without unit valuation retain unknown individual cost.

Mining uses a frozen opening ledger watermark and cumulative differences. Ambiguous daily baseline buckets are not retroactively counted. A Mining quantity creates stock, not sale revenue.

PI requires an own post-cutover factory/extractor recipe matching the newly observed asset type and a plausible upper output bound. Pins describe production configuration, not complete historical production transactions: this provenance stays rule based, with unknown full production cost.

Industry uses delivered post-cutover jobs and official blueprint output quantities to bound new observed stock. Upstream transformations retain lineage and known acquisition costs. A job does not prove free materials or introduce a tenth income category.

## Official catalogue rules

The public seed catalogues are derived from the official CCP static-data archive, with build, download URL and archive SHA256 recorded in each JSON file. Salvaged Materials can support Salvage. Ancient site materials and selected decryptor groups can support Hacking. Officer/deadspace modules can support inferred Loot. Common modules and ambiguous datacores are not categorical proof of NPC or site origin.

These decisions are labelled rule based in stock and income traces. Complete current Wallet, Assets, Market, Contracts, Mining, PI and Industry processing is required before a residual inference. Absence of a purchase is not mathematical proof of self-production.

## Transfers, corrections and exceptions

Unchanged physical item identity, type and quantity can prove an own-character transfer when both owners have successful observations. No cash or additional group stock is created. Confirmed explicit transfers and transformations bridge the existing provenance system to lots.

A unique exact whole provisional lot can adopt a later post-cutover purchase. Prior consumed shares and remaining cost are reallocated with cent conservation and audited before/after projections. Automated Contract splits can refresh when origin or explicit fee/tax evidence changes; manually confirmed or revoked splits remain protected.

Partial/multiple late matches and transferred/transformed descendants currently require an audited review rather than a partial correction or duplicate stock. Market FIFO is deferred when an existing manually confirmed position makes disposal ambiguous. Changed purchase evidence invalidates its old projected cost/category rather than continuing to show a proven gain. Non-reconstructed net quantity differences remain visible and do not create income. An explicitly incomplete opening disables residual type/PI/Industry inference for its original character scope: the first reliable stock observation cannot be falsely treated as new self-production. Direct actual purchases and Mining evidence can still be followed. Late historical purchases cannot create new post-opening type-derived income.
