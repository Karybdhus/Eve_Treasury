# Economic valuation and accounting policy

## Five different quantities

Wallet change is a liquidity observation. Asset value change combines quantity and market-price effects. Realized revenue comes from an actual payment. Known result deducts evidenced costs. Unrealized valuation change creates no income. None automatically releases PLEX budget.

## Prices

Use a saved unambiguous exact unit quote from existing manual or Janice valuation evidence. Store its source and observation time. Price times integer quantity is computed in cents. Stale, missing, conflicting or non-exact unit quotes stay unknown; missing opening prices do not block a complete quantity baseline. Opening prices and available names are frozen. Current prices belong to the current observation, not a mutation of the opening.

## Coverage and scope

The implemented wealth read model reports observed Wallet and personal Assets, known subtotals, unpriced item counts and comparable character scope. New and removed characters are scope changes, not income or expenses. The comparison uses the intersection of character scope and shows additions/removals separately.

Fresh complete personal Market-order endpoint versions can freeze and report actual Buy escrow separately. An empty captured response can prove zero; missing, stale, duplicate or malformed orders remain unknown. Escrow is not free Wallet liquidity. Sell-order goods, PI colony stock, deposits and external flows require independent consistent evidence; unavailable components are explicitly outside complete coverage. Orders are never added a second time to personal Assets. Container parents remain distinct physical item identities rather than copying child quantities. An incomplete valuation or active sell-order component prevents an exact overall wealth change.

Even complete Wallet/Asset prices establish a comparable subtotal, not overall wealth coverage. The UI reports their change as a partial comparison and keeps overall wealth change unknown. A complete cash-adjusted total wealth series and separate price/quantity decomposition are not claimed by this release. The presented asset-value difference is labelled valuation change, not realized gain.

## Payments and costs

Only the existing central economic events represent cash. Open orders and production quantities are not revenue. Own-character transfers are eliminated. PLEX purchases are capital conversion. OPENING_STOCK sale receipts are shown separately as opening-stock liquidation, outside the nine income categories. Unknown historical acquisition cost prevents a historical trading-profit claim.

Actual Contract and Market receipts are distributed once, preserving their full cent amount. A mixed same-type FIFO receipt can use a visibly estimated quantity distribution. Different-type mixed receipts require stored item valuation; missing values leave an honest unallocated remainder.

Trading purchase basis is consumed proportionally and retained through explicit lineage. Profit requires purchase basis plus explicit broker/fee and tax coverage; absence of fee rows does not prove zero. Included costs are not deducted again. Existing broker-refund corrections remain cost corrections rather than revenue. Mixed-origin fee allocation remains unknown unless supported by evidence.

## Evidence boundary

ESI snapshots and PI pins are not a complete transaction history. Missing intermediate stock observations can leave quantity gaps. Data age, observation window, source failures and rule-based origin are surfaced. Full live coverage must be verified before productive migration; copied historical replay cannot establish fresh observations.
